# Supreme Court Blocks Trump's Attempt to Fire Fed Governor Lisa Cook Temporarily

**NEWS** | 2026.06.30 | JustNow

The Supreme Court ruled 5-4 on June 29, 2026, that President Trump cannot fire Federal Reserve Governor Lisa Cook without due process, rejecting his bid to stay a lower court ruling. The decision underscores the Fed's independence from political interference.
