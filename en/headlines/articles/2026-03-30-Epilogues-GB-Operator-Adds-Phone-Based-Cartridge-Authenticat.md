# Epilogue's GB Operator Adds Phone-Based Cartridge Authentication, But Accuracy Varies

**NEWS** | 2026.03.30 | JustNow

In March 2026, Epilogue updated its $50 GB Operator device with a smartphone app called Retrace for Android and iOS. The new feature allows users to scan Game Boy, GBC, and GBA cartridges to authenticate them and check market value, though initial testing shows mixed reliability in detecting counterfeits.
