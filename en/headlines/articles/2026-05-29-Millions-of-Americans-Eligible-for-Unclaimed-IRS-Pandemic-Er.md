# Millions of Americans Eligible for Unclaimed IRS Pandemic-Era Refunds

**NEWS** | 2026.05.29 | JustNow

The IRS reports 1.2 million unclaimed refunds worth over $1.5 billion from 2021 tax year. Filers have until May 17, 2026, to claim the Recovery Rebate Credit, potentially receiving up to $3,200 each.
