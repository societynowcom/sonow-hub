# Founder Used AI to Navigate Rare Cancer Diagnosis and Treatment

**NEWS** | 2026.06.28 | JustNow

Connor Christou, 35, used Claude AI to analyze bloodwork, scan data, and journals after a rare lymphoma diagnosis. The model helped him choose aggressive chemo over a lighter regimen and avoid unnecessary radiotherapy by identifying thymus rebound, a 60% false-positive pitfall.
