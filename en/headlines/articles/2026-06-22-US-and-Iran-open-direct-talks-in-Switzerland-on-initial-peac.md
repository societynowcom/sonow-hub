# US and Iran open direct talks in Switzerland on initial peace deal

**NEWS** | 2026.06.22 | JustNow

US and Iranian officials started direct talks in Switzerland after signing an initial peace deal. The agreement includes a 60-day timeline for a final accord, a halt to fighting on all fronts, and reopening of the Strait of Hormuz.
