# Eleven dead after skydiving plane crashes in eastern France

**NEWS** | 2026.06.29 | JustNow

A light aircraft carrying skydivers crashed in Tomblaine, eastern France, killing the pilot and 10 passengers, including five students on their first dive. Officials said no bystanders were hurt.
