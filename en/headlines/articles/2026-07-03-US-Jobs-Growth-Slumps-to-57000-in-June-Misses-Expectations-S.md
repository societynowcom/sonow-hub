# US Jobs Growth Slumps to 57,000 in June, Misses Expectations Sharply

**NEWS** | 2026.07.03 | JustNow

US nonfarm payrolls rose by only 57,000 in June 2026, far below the 115,000 expected. The unemployment rate fell to 4.2% as labor force participation dropped to 61.5%, its lowest since March 2021.
