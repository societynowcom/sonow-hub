# IndexCache: New Sparse Attention Optimizer Boosts Long-Context AI Inference by 1.82x

**NEWS** | 2026.03.30 | JustNow

Researchers from Tsinghua University and Z.ai have developed IndexCache, a new optimization technique that reduces redundant computation in sparse attention AI models by up to 75%. The method achieves 1.82x faster initial response and 1.48x higher throughput for processing 200,000-token contexts, directly addressing the soaring cost and latency of long-context AI inference as of 2026.
