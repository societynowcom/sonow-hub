# Iran submits formal response to US proposals aimed at ending conflict

**NEWS** | 2026.05.11 | JustNow

Iran sent its response to US proposals to end the war via Pakistani mediators, state media reported. No details were disclosed. The US proposals reportedly include a 14-point memo on nuclear enrichment suspension, sanctions relief, and free transit through the Strait of Hormuz.
