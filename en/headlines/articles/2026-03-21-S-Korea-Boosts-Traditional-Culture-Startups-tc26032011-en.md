# S. Korea Boosts Traditional Culture Startups

**NEWS** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea’s Culture Ministry will provide up to ₩150 million (approx. $115,000 USD) to 31 youth-led startups preserving and innovating traditional arts. The initiative aims to revitalize cultural heritage and foster global expansion.
