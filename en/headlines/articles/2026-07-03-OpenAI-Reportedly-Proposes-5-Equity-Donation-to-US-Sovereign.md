# OpenAI Reportedly Proposes 5% Equity Donation to US Sovereign Wealth Fund

**NEWS** | 2026.07.03 | JustNow

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman proposed donating 5% of company equity to a US sovereign wealth fund, per the Financial Times. The plan, aimed at securing administration ties and addressing backlash, mirrors earlier discussions with President Trump. It requires congressional approval and faces competition from a more aggressive 50% tax bill by Senator Sanders.
