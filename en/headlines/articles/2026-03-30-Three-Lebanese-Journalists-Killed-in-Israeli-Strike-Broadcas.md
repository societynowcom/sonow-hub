# Three Lebanese Journalists Killed in Israeli Strike, Broadcasters Say

**NEWS** | 2026.03.30 | JustNow

Three Lebanese journalists from Al Manar and Al Mayadeen networks were killed in an Israeli strike in southern Lebanon. The IDF confirmed targeting one reporter it described as a Hezbollah operative. Lebanese officials condemned the attack as a violation of international law.
