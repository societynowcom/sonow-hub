# Pakistan Tanker First to Transit Strait of Hormuz

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

A Pakistani oil tanker, the Karachi, successfully passed through the Strait of Hormuz – effectively blocked by Iran – arriving in Karachi on Friday. India also reports its vessels transited with naval escorts, suggesting potential negotiations for safe passage.
