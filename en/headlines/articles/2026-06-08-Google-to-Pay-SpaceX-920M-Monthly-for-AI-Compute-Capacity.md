# Google to Pay SpaceX $920M Monthly for AI Compute Capacity

**NEWS** | 2026.06.08 | JustNow

Google will pay SpaceX $920M monthly from Oct 2026 for 110,000 NVIDIA GPUs, citing unexpected demand for its Gemini Enterprise AI platform. The deal includes a cancellation clause after Dec 2026.
