# Korea Launches National Stray Animal Hotline

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea launched a dedicated hotline (1577-0954) on March 18th to report lost or abandoned animals. The service aims to streamline rescue efforts and improve animal welfare nationwide, with 24/7 online reporting also available.
