# Nvidia and Corning Forge $2.7 Billion Optical Fiber Deal to Supercharge AI Infrastructure

**NEWS** | 2026.05.09 | JustNow

Nvidia partners with Corning on three new U.S. optical fiber factories, investing up to $2.7 billion. The deal aims to replace copper cables with glass fiber in AI systems, boosting speed and cutting energy use by up to 20x.
