# Uganda's Museveni sworn in for seventh term after disputed election

**NEWS** | 2026.05.13 | JustNow

Ugandan President Yoweri Museveni, 81, was sworn in for a seventh consecutive term on Wednesday after winning January's disputed elections, extending his 40-year rule. Heavy security was deployed in Kampala as opposition leader Bobi Wine rejected the results.
