# Hyundai/Kia Boosts N. America Output to 95% Amid Tariff Concerns

**NEWS** | 2026.03.25 | JustNow

Hyundai Motor Group is ramping up production in North America, exceeding 95% capacity. This move aims to circumvent tariffs imposed by the U.S. Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) and expand market share. Output is projected to reach 2.4 million vehicles by 2026.
