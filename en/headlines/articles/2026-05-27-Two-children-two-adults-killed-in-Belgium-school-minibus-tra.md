# Two children, two adults killed in Belgium school minibus-train collision

**NEWS** | 2026.05.27 | JustNow

Two children aged 12 and 15, a driver and a chaperone died when a school minibus collided with a train in Buggenhout, Belgium. The barrier was reportedly down at the time of the crash. Five other children are in critical condition.
