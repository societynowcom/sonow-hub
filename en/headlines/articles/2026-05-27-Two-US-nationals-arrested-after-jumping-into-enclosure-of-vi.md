# Two US nationals arrested after jumping into enclosure of viral monkey Punch at Japanese zoo

**NEWS** | 2026.05.27 | JustNow

Two US nationals were arrested in Japan after one jumped into the enclosure of Punch, a viral baby macaque, while the other filmed. Police said no monkeys were hurt. The zoo is now considering a filming ban.
