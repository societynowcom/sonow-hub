# S. Korea Parties Agree to Add Pro-Democracy Movements to Constitution

**정책브리핑** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

Six South Korean political parties agreed to include the May 18th Democratic Uprising and the Busan-Masan Democratic Struggle in the preamble of the Constitution. This aims to reaffirm the historical value of these movements, but faces hurdles due to the absence of a key opposition party.
