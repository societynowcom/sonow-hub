# France swelters through hottest day on record as power cuts hit thousands

**NEWS** | 2026.06.25 | JustNow

France recorded its hottest day since records began in 1947, with the national temperature indicator hitting 30C. Power outages affected tens of thousands of homes in western France as temperatures in Paris and other areas broke 40C on Wednesday.
