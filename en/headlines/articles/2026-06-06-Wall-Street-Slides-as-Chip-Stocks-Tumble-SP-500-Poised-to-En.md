# Wall Street Slides as Chip Stocks Tumble, S&P 500 Poised to End 9-Week Win Streak

**NEWS** | 2026.06.06 | JustNow

U.S. stocks fell sharply on June 5, 2026, as chip stocks plunged and a stronger-than-expected jobs report boosted Treasury yields. The Nasdaq lost 3.1%, while the S&P 500 dropped 1.9%, ending its nine-week winning streak. Broadcom, Marvell, and Micron led declines, with consumer staples gaining as investors rotated out of tech.
