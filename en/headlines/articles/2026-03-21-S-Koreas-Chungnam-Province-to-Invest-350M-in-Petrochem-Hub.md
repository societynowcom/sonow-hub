# S. Korea's Chungnam Province to Invest $350M in Petrochem Hub

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Chungcheong Province will invest 464.4 billion won ($350M USD) to revitalize the Daesan Petrochemical Complex, facing challenges from global oversupply and Middle East instability. The plan includes wage subsidies for 5,350 workers and long-term investments in AI and green industries.
