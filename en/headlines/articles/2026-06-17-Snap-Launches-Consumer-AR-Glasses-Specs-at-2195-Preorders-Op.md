# Snap Launches Consumer AR Glasses Specs at $2,195, Preorders Open

**NEWS** | 2026.06.17 | JustNow

Snap launches Specs, its first consumer AR glasses, at $2,195 with preorders starting June 2026. The standalone wearable features dual Snapdragon processors, 51-degree field of view, and ships in the US, UK, and France this fall.
