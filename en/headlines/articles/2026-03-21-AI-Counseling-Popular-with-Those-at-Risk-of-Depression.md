# AI Counseling Popular with Those at Risk of Depression

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

A study shows AI counseling is twice as popular among those at high risk of depression (53%) compared to those with normal mental health (27%). 77% of respondents expressed willingness to use AI counseling in the future.
