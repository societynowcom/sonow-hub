# Board, the game startup from Mirror founder Brynn Putnam, secures $20M Series A

**NEWS** | 2026.06.03 | JustNow

Board, a startup from Mirror founder Brynn Putnam, raised $20M in Series A funding led by Union Square Ventures. The company's 24-inch touchscreen device blends physical board games with digital interactivity, selling tens of thousands of units across 50 states. A new AI platform lets users create games via natural language prompts.
