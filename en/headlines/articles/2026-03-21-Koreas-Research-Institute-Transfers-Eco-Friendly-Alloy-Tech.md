# Korea's Research Institute Transfers Eco-Friendly Alloy Tech

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

Korea's Production Technology Research Institute (KI) transferred rights to its 'Eco-Almag' aluminum alloy tech to a private firm for ₩3 billion. The eco-friendly alloy aims to reduce import reliance and bolster strategic material supply.
