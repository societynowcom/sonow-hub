# Busan Chamber of Commerce Boosts On-Site Checks Amid Middle East Crisis

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

The Busan Chamber of Commerce and Industry (BCCI) is expanding on-site inspections of local businesses due to concerns over the prolonged Middle East conflict. The inspections aim to assess the impact of rising logistics costs and supply chain disruptions.
