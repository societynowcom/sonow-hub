# US Existing Home Sales Slip in June as Median Price Hits Record $440,600

**NEWS** | 2026.07.10 | JustNow

US existing home sales fell 2.4% in June from May to 4.09 million units, missing forecasts. Median price hit a record $440,600, up 1.8% year-over-year. Inventory remains tight at 1.56 million units, while all-cash deals dropped to 25% of transactions.
