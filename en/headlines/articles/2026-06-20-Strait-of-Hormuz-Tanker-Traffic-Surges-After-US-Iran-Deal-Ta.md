# Strait of Hormuz Tanker Traffic Surges After U.S.-Iran Deal Takes Effect

**NEWS** | 2026.06.20 | JustNow

Oil tanker traffic in the Strait of Hormuz surged to 20 vessels on Thursday, the highest since June 2, after the U.S. and Iran implemented a deal to reopen the sea lane without tolls for 60 days.
