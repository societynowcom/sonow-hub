# Zest Launches Restaurant Discovery App Using Real Transaction Data and AI

**NEWS** | 2026.06.11 | JustNow

Zest, a new restaurant discovery app, uses credit card transaction data and AI to recommend dining spots based on where users actually eat. Backed by $1.8M from Alexis Ohanian’s 776 and Kindred Ventures, it launched publicly after beta testing. Users link cards via Plaid to create a dining map, with over 100,000 visits post-launch.
