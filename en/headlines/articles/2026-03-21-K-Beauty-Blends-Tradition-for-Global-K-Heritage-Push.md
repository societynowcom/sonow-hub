# K-Beauty Blends Tradition for Global 'K-Heritage' Push

**NEWS** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea’s K-beauty industry is increasingly incorporating traditional Korean culture – from herbal medicine to ceramics – to appeal to global consumers. This 'K-Heritage' strategy aims to share Korean artistry and identity, driving demand for modern interpretations of tradition.
