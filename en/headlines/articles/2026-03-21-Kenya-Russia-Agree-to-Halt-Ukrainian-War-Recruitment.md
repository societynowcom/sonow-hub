# Kenya, Russia Agree to Halt Ukrainian War Recruitment

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

Kenya and Russia reached an agreement to stop the recruitment of Kenyan citizens into the war in Ukraine. Approximately 1,000 Kenyans are believed to have been recruited, with discussions underway for their repatriation.
