# SpaceX IPO Total Reaches $85.7 Billion After Underwriters Exercise Greenshoe Option

**NEWS** | 2026.06.16 | JustNow

SpaceX raised $85.7 billion in its IPO after underwriters exercised the greenshoe option, buying 83.3 million additional shares at $135 each. The stock surged 19% on debut, valuing the company at over $2 trillion.
