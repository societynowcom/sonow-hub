# SpaceX IPO swells to $85.7 billion after underwriters exercise share option

**NEWS** | 2026.06.16 | JustNow

SpaceX's IPO grew to $85.7 billion after underwriters exercised their full share option, making it the largest public offering ever. The company will use funds to reduce debt tied to X and xAI, expand AI compute, and upgrade Starlink and launch infrastructure.
