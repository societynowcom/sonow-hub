# S. Korea's President Proposes Constitutional Revision

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korean President Lee Jae-myung proposed adding the Buma Resistance to the preamble of the constitution, alongside the May 18 Democratic Movement. He urged government review of constitutional amendments and suggested a phased approach.
