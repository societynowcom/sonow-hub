# German court sentences Christmas market attacker to life imprisonment for 2024 Magdeburg rampage

**NEWS** | 2026.06.27 | JustNow

A German court sentenced Taleb Al-Abdulmohsen to life in prison for driving a BMW into a crowded Christmas market in Magdeburg in 2024, killing six and injuring 300. The 51-year-old Saudi national was convicted of murder.
