# Ukrainian drones strike St. Petersburg oil depots as Putin prepares for economic forum

**NEWS** | 2026.06.04 | JustNow

Ukrainian drones struck oil storage facilities near St. Petersburg on Wednesday, hours before Vladimir Putin was set to address a major economic forum. Local authorities said 59 drones were shot down, but three districts were hit, causing black smoke to billow over the city and temporarily closing Pulkovo Airport.
