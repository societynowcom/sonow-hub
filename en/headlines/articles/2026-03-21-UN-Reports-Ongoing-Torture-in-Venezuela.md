# UN Reports Ongoing Torture in Venezuela

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

The UN human rights office reports continued torture of detainees in Venezuela despite a government claim of releasing 7,700 prisoners. Independent groups confirm fewer than 700 releases, with over 500 held for political reasons.
