# 30-Year US Treasury Yield Surges to 5.198%, Highest Since 2007

**NEWS** | 2026.05.27 | JustNow

The 30-year US Treasury yield hit 5.198%, its highest since July 2007, as investors flee bonds amid renewed inflation fears and rising oil prices linked to the Iran conflict. The 10-year yield rose to 4.687%.
