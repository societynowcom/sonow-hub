# JetBlue Slashes Newark and LaGuardia Operations to Boost Florida Hub

**NEWS** | 2026.06.18 | JustNow

JetBlue will close flight attendant and tech bases at Newark and LaGuardia by fall 2026, redeploying staff to Fort Lauderdale. The carrier aims to cut costs and boost profitable Florida routes, including new cross-country Mint service.
