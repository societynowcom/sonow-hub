# S&P 500 Slumps as Global Chip Rout Deepens; Nasdaq Drops 1.5%

**NEWS** | 2026.06.24 | JustNow

The S&P 500 fell 1% on June 23, 2026, as a global semiconductor rout deepened. Memory chip stocks led declines, with SK Hynix down 12% and Micron falling 10%. The Nasdaq Composite slid 1.5%, while the Dow gained 118 points.
