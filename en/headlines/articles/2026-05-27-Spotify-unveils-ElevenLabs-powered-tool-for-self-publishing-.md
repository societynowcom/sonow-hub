# Spotify unveils ElevenLabs-powered tool for self-publishing audiobooks

**NEWS** | 2026.05.27 | JustNow

Spotify announced an ElevenLabs-powered audiobook creation tool for authors, launching in beta June 2026. The feature, part of Spotify for Authors, allows AI-narrated books without exclusivity. The platform also expands language support and Audiobook+ plans, having surpassed 1 million subscriptions.
