# S&P 500, Nasdaq Rise as Oil Wavers and Chip Stocks Rebound; Iran Diplomacy Hopes Emerge

**NEWS** | 2026.07.21 | JustNow

U.S. stocks rose Monday as oil prices fluctuated amid U.S.-Iran strikes, with chipmakers leading gains. The S&P 500 added 0.3%, the Nasdaq climbed 0.6%, while the Dow fell 158 points. Hopes for diplomacy emerged after Iran signaled openness to negotiations.
