# S. Korea Invests $6M to Secure Water for Farms

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea will invest 8 billion won ($6 million USD) to bolster agricultural water supplies ahead of the planting season. The initiative targets 78 drought-prone areas across 11 provinces, aiming to irrigate 1,011 hectares of farmland.
