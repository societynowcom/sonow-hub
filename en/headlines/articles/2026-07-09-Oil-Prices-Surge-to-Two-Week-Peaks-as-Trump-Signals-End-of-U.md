# Oil Prices Surge to Two-Week Peaks as Trump Signals End of U.S.-Iran Cease-Fire

**NEWS** | 2026.07.09 | JustNow

Oil prices rose to two-week highs after President Trump indicated the U.S.-Iran cease-fire is over, stoking supply disruption fears. Brent crude climbed 2.3% to $78.45 per barrel, while WTI gained 2.1% to $74.30. The move reflects heightened geopolitical risk in the Middle East, a region accounting for nearly 30% of global oil output. Analysts warn of potential volatility ahead.
