# S. Korea to Fund Traditional Culture Startups

**NEWS** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea’s Culture Ministry will launch a program in 2026 to support startups in the traditional culture sector. The initiative will provide funding and mentorship to promote modernization and industrialization of Korean heritage.
