# S. Korea Housing Sentiment Falls in February

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea's housing market sentiment declined in February, with the nationwide index dropping to 99 from 102 in January. The shift signals a pause after a period of growth, influenced by changes in property tax policies.
