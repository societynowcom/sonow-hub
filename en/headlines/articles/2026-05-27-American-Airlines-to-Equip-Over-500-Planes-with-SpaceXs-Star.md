# American Airlines to Equip Over 500 Planes with SpaceX's Starlink for In-Flight Wi-Fi

**NEWS** | 2026.05.27 | JustNow

American Airlines will install SpaceX's Starlink Wi-Fi on over 500 narrow-body Airbus jets starting 2027, competing with Delta's Amazon Leo deal. Starlink has won contracts with United, Southwest, and Alaska Airlines.
