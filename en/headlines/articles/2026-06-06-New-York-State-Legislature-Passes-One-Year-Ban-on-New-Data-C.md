# New York State Legislature Passes One-Year Ban on New Data Centers

**NEWS** | 2026.06.06 | JustNow

New York lawmakers passed a one-year ban on large data centers over 20MW, requiring impact studies on energy, water, and pollution. Governor Hochul has until December to sign or veto the bill, which faces industry pushback.
