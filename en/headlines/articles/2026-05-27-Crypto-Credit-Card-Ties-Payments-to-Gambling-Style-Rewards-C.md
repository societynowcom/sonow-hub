# Crypto Credit Card Ties Payments to Gambling-Style Rewards, Critics Warn

**NEWS** | 2026.05.27 | JustNow

A new crypto-linked credit card uses gambling-style rewards, including variable cashback and lottery-like bonuses, to incentivize spending. Critics say it blurs the line between finance and gambling, potentially harming vulnerable users. The card, launched in 2026, reportedly ties rewards to market volatility, creating unpredictable outcomes for consumers.
