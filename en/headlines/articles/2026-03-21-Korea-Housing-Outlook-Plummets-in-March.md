# Korea Housing Outlook Plummets in March

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea's housing business sentiment fell sharply in March, hitting 89.0, down 6.8 points from the previous month. The decline reflects increased supply of high-end homes in Seoul and cautious buyer behavior.
