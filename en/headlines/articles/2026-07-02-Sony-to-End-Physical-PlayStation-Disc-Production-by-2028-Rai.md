# Sony to End Physical PlayStation Disc Production by 2028, Raising Preservation Concerns

**NEWS** | 2026.07.02 | JustNow

Sony will cease physical PlayStation disc production in January 2028, shifting to digital-only sales. The move, alongside PS3 and PS Vita store closures, highlights risks for game preservation as 80% of PS5 sales are already digital.
