# Sony Sells Majority Stake in TV Business to TCL in $473 Million Joint Venture

**NEWS** | 2026.04.01 | JustNow

Sony transfers 51% stake in its TV business to TCL for $473M, creating Bravia Inc. joint venture. New entity will combine Sony's branding with TCL's manufacturing scale starting April 2027.
