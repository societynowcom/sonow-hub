# China's Worst Mining Disaster in Over a Decade Kills 82 at Shanxi Coal Mine

**NEWS** | 2026.05.27 | JustNow

A gas explosion at the Liushenyu Coal Mine in Shanxi killed 82 people, with two missing. It is China's worst mining disaster since 2009. President Xi Jinping ordered all efforts for rescue. Robots with gas sensors were deployed. The BBC reported from the scene.
