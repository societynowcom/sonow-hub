# Dodger Stadium to Rebrand After 64 Years

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

Los Angeles' Dodger Stadium will change its name to 'UNIQLO Field at Dodger Stadium' in 2026. The deal with Japanese apparel brand UNIQLO reflects the team's growing ties with Japanese companies since signing Shohei Ohtani.
