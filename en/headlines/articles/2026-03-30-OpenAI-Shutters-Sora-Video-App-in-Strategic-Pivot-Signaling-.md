# OpenAI Shutters Sora Video App in Strategic Pivot, Signaling AI Video Reality Check

**NEWS** | 2026.03.30 | JustNow

OpenAI is shutting down its Sora AI video generation app and related models after just six months, according to a March 2026 announcement. The move signals a strategic refocus on enterprise tools and a broader industry reassessment of generative video's near-term viability, amid parallel delays at ByteDance.
