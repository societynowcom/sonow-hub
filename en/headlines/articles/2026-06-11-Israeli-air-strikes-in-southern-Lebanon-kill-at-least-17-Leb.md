# Israeli air strikes in southern Lebanon kill at least 17, Lebanese media report

**NEWS** | 2026.06.11 | JustNow

Israeli air strikes killed at least 17 people in southern Lebanon on Wednesday, Lebanese state media reported, including nine in the town of Tayr Debba. Hezbollah said it attacked Israeli troops in the south. The strikes came as Iran warned Israel to halt its campaign against the militant group.
