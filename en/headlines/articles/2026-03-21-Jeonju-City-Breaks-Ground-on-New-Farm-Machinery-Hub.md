# Jeonju City Breaks Ground on New Farm Machinery Hub

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

Jeonju City, South Korea, began construction on a new agricultural machinery rental center branch to serve farmers in the southern region. The 657㎡ facility will house over 100 machines, improving access & reducing costs.
