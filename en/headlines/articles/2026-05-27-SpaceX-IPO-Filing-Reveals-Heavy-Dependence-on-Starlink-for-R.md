# SpaceX IPO Filing Reveals Heavy Dependence on Starlink for Revenue and Profit

**NEWS** | 2026.05.27 | JustNow

SpaceX's IPO prospectus shows Starlink generated $11.39B in revenue in 2025, 61% of total sales. The satellite internet unit is the company's only profitable division, earning $4.42B while AI lost $6.35B and launches lost $657M.
