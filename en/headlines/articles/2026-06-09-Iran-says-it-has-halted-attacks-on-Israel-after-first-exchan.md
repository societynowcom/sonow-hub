# Iran says it has halted attacks on Israel after first exchange of fire since truce

**NEWS** | 2026.06.09 | JustNow

Iran says it has stopped military operations against Israel after launching about 30 missiles and Israel responding with two waves of air strikes, the first such exchange since an April ceasefire. US President Donald Trump called for an immediate halt to hostilities.
