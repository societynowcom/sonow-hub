# Brazil Enacts Landmark Law Granting Pets Joint Custody Rights in Divorce Cases

**NEWS** | 2026.04.03 | JustNow

Brazil's Congress passes groundbreaking law allowing judges to award shared custody of pets in divorce cases. The legislation reflects pets' status as family members in the country of 213 million people with 160 million pets.
