# Amazon search bar now shows AI-generated product images you cannot purchase

**NEWS** | 2026.06.04 | JustNow

Amazon's app now generates AI images of clothing and home goods as you type descriptions, but these products are not real or purchasable. The feature helps users find similar real items by selecting the AI-generated image that matches their vision. Available on iOS and Android, it follows Google's AI Mode launch last year.
