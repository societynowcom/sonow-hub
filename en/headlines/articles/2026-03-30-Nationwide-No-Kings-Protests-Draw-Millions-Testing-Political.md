# Nationwide 'No Kings' Protests Draw Millions, Testing Political Climate Ahead of Midterms

**NEWS** | 2026.03.30 | JustNow

On March 28, 2026, over 3,200 'No Kings' protest events occurred across all 50 U.S. states, constituting what organizers call the largest single-day nonviolent protest in U.S. history. Millions demonstrated against President Donald Trump's policies, with events from New York to Los Angeles highlighting immigration, foreign policy, and concerns over democratic norms ahead of the 2026 midterm elections.
