# US Supreme Court upholds bans on transgender women in female school and college sports

**NEWS** | 2026.07.01 | JustNow

The US Supreme Court ruled states can ban transgender women from female school and college sports, upholding laws in Idaho and West Virginia. The 9-0 decision on civil rights law but split on constitutional grounds affects over 20 states with similar bans.
