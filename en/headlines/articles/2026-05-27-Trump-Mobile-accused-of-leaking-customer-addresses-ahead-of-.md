# Trump Mobile accused of leaking customer addresses ahead of T1 Phone launch

**NEWS** | 2026.05.27 | JustNow

Trump Mobile allegedly exposed customer addresses and phone numbers via a website vulnerability. YouTubers Coffeezilla and penguinz0 confirmed their data was in the leaked pre-order database, which reportedly contains roughly 10,000 unique customers and 30,000 orders—far below viral claims of 590,000.
