# Bluesky Launches Attie, an AI Agent for User-Controlled Social Feeds

**NEWS** | 2026.03.30 | JustNow

Bluesky has launched Attie, a standalone AI app that lets users build custom social feeds using natural language commands via Anthropic's Claude. Announced at the Atmosphere conference in March 2026, the tool represents a strategic shift towards agentic, user-controlled algorithms on the open AT Protocol, following a $100 million funding round that secures the company's multi-year runway.
