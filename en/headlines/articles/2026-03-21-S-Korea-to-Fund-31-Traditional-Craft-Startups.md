# S. Korea to Fund 31 Traditional Craft Startups

**NEWS** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea’s Culture Ministry will support 31 traditional craft startups in 2026, aiming to modernize and commercialize heritage techniques. The initiative focuses on blending artistry with consumer needs and fostering a sustainable ecosystem.
