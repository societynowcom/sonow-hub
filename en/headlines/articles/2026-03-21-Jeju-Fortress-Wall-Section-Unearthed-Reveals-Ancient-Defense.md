# Jeju Fortress Wall Section Unearthed, Reveals Ancient Defense

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

A 84-meter section of Jeju Fortress, a UNESCO World Heritage site, has been discovered in Jeju City. The find includes intact 'yeojang' defensive structures—a first for the fortress—offering new insights into Joseon Dynasty military architecture.
