# Israel-Lebanon Ceasefire Takes Effect After Six-Week Conflict, But Long-Term Peace Uncertain

**NEWS** | 2026.05.09 | JustNow

A 10-day ceasefire between Israel and Hezbollah has taken effect after six weeks of conflict that killed over 2,100 people and displaced one million Lebanese. The truce brings temporary relief but key issues including Israeli troop withdrawal and Hezbollah disarmament remain unresolved.
