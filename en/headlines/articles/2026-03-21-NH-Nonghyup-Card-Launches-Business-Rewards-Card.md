# NH Nonghyup Card Launches Business Rewards Card

**HEADLINES** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

NH Nonghyup Card released the 'SOHO add' card for small business owners on March 17th. The card offers up to 1.1% rewards and an additional 10% bonus for spending over ₩20 million annually.
