# Meta to Power $800M AI Data Center with 100MW Solar Farm

**AI** | 2026.03.29 | JustNow

Meta has agreed to a $100 million deal with Silicon Ranch to build a 100MW solar facility in South Carolina. The plant will power Meta’s new $800 million AI data center, with most equipment sourced domestically.
