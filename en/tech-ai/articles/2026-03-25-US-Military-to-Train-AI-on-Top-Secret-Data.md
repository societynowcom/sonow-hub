# US Military to Train AI on Top Secret Data

**AI** | 2026.03.25 | JustNow

The US Department of Defense is exploring allowing AI firms access to classified military data for AI model training. This aims to enhance AI capabilities for tasks like target analysis, but raises security concerns. The initiative is in early stages, with secure data centers planned.
