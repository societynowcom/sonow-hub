# South Korea Launches 2026 Smart City Initiative

**디지털트윈** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea’s Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT) has begun accepting proposals for its 2026 Smart City Development Project. The initiative aims to support local governments in digitally transforming urban areas, with funding and tech infrastructure allocated to selected regions.
