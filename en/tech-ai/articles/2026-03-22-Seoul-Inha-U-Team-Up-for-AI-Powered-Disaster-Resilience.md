# Seoul & Inha U. Team Up for AI-Powered Disaster Resilience

**디지털트윈** | 2026.03.22 | JustNow

A joint research effort between Inha University and Seoul National University is developing a digital twin technology to boost South Korea’s urban disaster response. The project aims to predict and minimize damage in Seoul, with a ₩1 billion budget over five years.
