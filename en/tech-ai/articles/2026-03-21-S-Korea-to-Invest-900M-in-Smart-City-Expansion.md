# S. Korea to Invest $900M in Smart City Expansion

**디지털트윈** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea plans to invest 1.2 trillion won ($900M USD) by 2025 to expand smart city initiatives to 30 cities nationwide. The focus is on leveraging digital twin technology for improvements in traffic, energy, and safety.
