# Aviva's Digital Twin Boosts Nvidia AI Factory Efficiency

**디지털트윈** | 2026.03.22 | JustNow

South Korean firm Aviva is deploying its 'Lifecycle Digital Twin' architecture for Nvidia's gigawatt-scale AI factory project. This solution aims to optimize energy efficiency and reduce costs by virtually managing the AI data center's entire lifecycle, anticipating a 20% energy reduction.
