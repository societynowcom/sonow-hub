# Busan Extends Digital Twin Partnership to Build ‘Smart City’

**디지털트윈** | 2026.03.22 | JustNow

Busan, South Korea, is extending its partnership with LX Space Information Cluster for three more years to enhance its 3D digital twin platform. This collaboration aims to address urban challenges and improve citizen services, driving the city’s ‘Smart Busan’ initiative.
