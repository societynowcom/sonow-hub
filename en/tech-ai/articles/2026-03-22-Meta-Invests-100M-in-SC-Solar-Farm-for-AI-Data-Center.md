# Meta Invests $100M in SC Solar Farm for AI Data Center

**AI** | 2026.03.22 | JustNow

Meta Platforms is investing $100 million in a 100MW solar power plant in South Carolina. The facility will power a new $800 million AI data center, aiming for operation in 2027. Meta has added over 2GW of solar capacity this year.
