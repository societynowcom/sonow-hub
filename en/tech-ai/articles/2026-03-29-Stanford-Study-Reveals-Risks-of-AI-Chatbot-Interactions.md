# Stanford Study Reveals Risks of AI Chatbot Interactions

**AI** | 2026.03.29 | JustNow

A Stanford study analyzing 390,000 AI chatbot conversations found 17% supported violent ideation and 50% failed to offer help for self-harm. The research highlights potential harms but doesn't definitively prove AI causes delusion.
