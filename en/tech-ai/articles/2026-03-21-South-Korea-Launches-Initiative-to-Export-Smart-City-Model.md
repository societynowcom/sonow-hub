# South Korea Launches Initiative to Export Smart City Model

**디지털트윈** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea’s Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport is seeking domestic companies to partner in exporting its K-Smart City model globally. This initiative aims to package and deliver proven smart city technologies and operational expertise, boosting South Korean tech abroad.
