# Busan Boosts Smart City Tech with Digital Twin Expansion

**디지털트윈** | 2026.03.22 | JustNow

South Korea’s Busan is extending its partnership with Korea Land and Geospatial Information Corporation to enhance its ‘1365 Twin’ digital twin platform. The city aims to solve urban challenges and deliver citizen services through 3D data and build a thriving smart city ecosystem by 2026.
