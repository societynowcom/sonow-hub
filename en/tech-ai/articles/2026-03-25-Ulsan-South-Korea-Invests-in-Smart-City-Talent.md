# Ulsan, South Korea Invests in Smart City Talent

**디지털트윈** | 2026.03.25 | JustNow

Ulsan, South Korea is launching a $3.2M (5 billion KRW) initiative to train a skilled workforce for its planned ‘Hub-Type Smart City’ by 2026. The program focuses on practical skills in data analysis, IoT, and AI to boost urban competitiveness.
