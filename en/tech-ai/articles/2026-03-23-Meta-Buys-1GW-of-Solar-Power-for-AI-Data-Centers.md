# Meta Buys 1GW of Solar Power for AI Data Centers

**AI** | 2026.03.23 | JustNow

Meta has secured 1 gigawatt (GW) of solar power in Texas & Louisiana to fuel its growing AI data center needs. The move aims to offset the surge in electricity demand from AI development with renewable energy sources, exceeding 3GW total solar capacity by 2025.
