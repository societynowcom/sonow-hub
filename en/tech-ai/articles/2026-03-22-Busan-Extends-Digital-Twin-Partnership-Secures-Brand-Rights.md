# Busan Extends Digital Twin Partnership, Secures Brand Rights

**디지털트윈** | 2026.03.22 | JustNow

The city of Busan, South Korea, has extended its digital twin collaboration with LX for three years and secured exclusive rights to the ‘1365 Twin’ brand. This aims to enhance smart city data platforms and deliver optimized digital twin services to citizens.
