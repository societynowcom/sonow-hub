# Korea Maritime Univ. to Build Smart City Testbed

**디지털트윈** | 2026.03.30 | JustNow

Korea Maritime University (KMU) has been selected for South Korea’s Smart City Special Zone program. By 2026, KMU will transform its campus into a living lab for marine-focused smart city technologies, aiming to solve urban challenges.
