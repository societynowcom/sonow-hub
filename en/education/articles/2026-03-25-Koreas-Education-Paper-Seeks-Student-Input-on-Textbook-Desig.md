# Korea's Education Paper Seeks Student Input on Textbook Design

**교육** | 2026.03.25 | JustNow

The Korea Education Newspaper is launching an event allowing 2026 Suneung (college entrance exam) test-takers to contribute to the design of the EBS Su능완성 (EBS Complete) textbook cover. This initiative aims to boost student engagement and improve learning resources.
