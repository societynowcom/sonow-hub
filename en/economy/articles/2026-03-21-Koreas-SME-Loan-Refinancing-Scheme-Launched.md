# Korea's SME Loan Refinancing Scheme Launched

**경제** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korea launched a program allowing small business owners to refinance loans at lower rates. Five major banks are offering an average 1.5% interest rate reduction, up to a 500 million won limit, with no transfer fees.
