# Korea Considers Revisiting Financial Investment Tax

**경제** | 2026.03.23 | JustNow

South Korea may reconsider a proposed financial investment tax (FIT) in 2026, sparking concerns about market volatility. The tax aims to curb tax evasion by the wealthy but faced prior opposition due to potential market disruption.
