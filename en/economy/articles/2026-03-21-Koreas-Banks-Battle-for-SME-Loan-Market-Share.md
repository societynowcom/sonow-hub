# Korea's Banks Battle for SME Loan Market Share

**경제** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

South Korean banks are fiercely competing for '사장님대출' (business owner loans) as tighter mortgage regulations drive demand. Major banks are expanding loan limits and cutting rates, with some offering rates as low as the mid-3% range.
