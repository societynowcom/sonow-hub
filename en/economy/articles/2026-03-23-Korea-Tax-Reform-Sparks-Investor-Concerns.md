# Korea Tax Reform Sparks Investor Concerns

**경제** | 2026.03.23 | JustNow

South Korea's potential reinstatement of a financial investment income tax (FIIT) is facing strong opposition from investors. Concerns are rising that the market instability seen during the 2023 FIIT debate could resurface, with a petition against the reform exceeding 100,000 signatures.
