# Korea Holds Rates Amidst Iran Tensions & Inflation

**경제** | 2026.03.21 | JustNow

The Bank of Korea is likely to hold its benchmark interest rate at 3.50% in April due to heightened geopolitical risks in the Middle East and resulting inflationary pressures. Discussions of rate cuts are postponed until uncertainties subside.
