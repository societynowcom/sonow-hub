# Gyeongui Line Forest Park, Yeonnam: Linear Urban Oasis with Indie Stores and Street Musicians

**Hidden Korea** | 2026.03.15 | SO,NOW

Gyeongui Line Forest Park in Yeonnam transforms a former 6.3-kilometer railway line into Seoul's innovative linear park with picnic lawns, children's play zones, and cultural spaces. The unique 30-50 meter-wide corridor connects multiple neighborhoods while supporting indie stores and street musicians that define the area's vibrant community character.
