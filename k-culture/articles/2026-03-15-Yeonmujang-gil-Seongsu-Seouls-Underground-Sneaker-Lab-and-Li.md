# Yeonmujang-gil, Seongsu: Seoul's Underground Sneaker Lab and Lifestyle Shopping Lane

**Hidden Korea** | 2026.03.15 | SO,NOW

Yeonmujang-gil in Seongsu is a 200-meter lane housing Korea's innovative sneaker laboratories and experimental lifestyle brands that operate on limited-edition drop culture. This underground shopping destination attracts serious collectors through exclusive releases, insider networks, and brands prioritizing craftsmanship over mass production.
