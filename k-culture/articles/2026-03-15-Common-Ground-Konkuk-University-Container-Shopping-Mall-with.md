# Common Ground Konkuk University: Container Shopping Mall with K-Fashion Brands and Pop-Up Events

**Hidden Korea** | 2026.03.15 | SO,NOW

Common Ground near Konkuk University is a shopping complex built from repurposed shipping containers, featuring independent Korean fashion brands, emerging designers, and rotating pop-up events. The modular container design provides affordable retail space for K-street fashion brands and creates a dynamic cultural hub for Seoul's fashion community.
